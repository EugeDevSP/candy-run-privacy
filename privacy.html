Privacy Policy

Candy Run · Last updated: July 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Candy Run (the “App”) handles your information. Candy Run is designed to respect your privacy: we do not collect, store, or share any personal data.

Information We Collect

None. Candy Run does not collect personal information. There are no user accounts, no sign-up, no analytics or advertising software, and no tracking. We do not have servers that receive data from the App.

Game Center

If you choose to sign in to Apple’s Game Center, the App submits your game score to a leaderboard so you can compare your ranking with other players. This feature is provided and operated by Apple. Any data involved (such as your Game Center nickname and scores) is handled by Apple under Apple’s Privacy Policy, not by us, and we do not receive or store it. Game Center is optional—if you do not sign in, no scores are submitted.

Game Progress & High Scores

Your high score and game settings are stored on your device. If you have iCloud enabled, this information may sync across your own devices through Apple’s iCloud service so your progress follows you. This data stays within your personal Apple account and is never transmitted to us.

Children’s Privacy

Candy Run is family-friendly and suitable for all ages. Because the App does not collect any personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect information from children.

Third Parties

Candy Run does not include third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking services. The only external service is Apple’s Game Center (optional), as described above.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
