Frequently Asked Questions

How do I play?
Move to catch the falling candy and treats, and avoid the rocks dropped by the eagles. Catch a heart to go invincible and smash rocks for bonus points. Build a combo streak to multiply your score!

How do leaderboards work?
Sign in to Game Center to submit your best score and see how you rank against players around the world. Game Center is optional.

Does the game cost anything?
Candy Run is free to play, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

Is my data collected?
No. See our Privacy Policy for details.
